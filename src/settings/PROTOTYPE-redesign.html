Beckon Settings Redesign

The last pass removed decoration and got plain instead of minimal, because it removed contrast along with the ornament. Minimalism is not the absence of signal — it is fewer elements, each carrying more. Three structurally different windows below, each paintable in three palettes. Flip with the bar at the bottom or the arrow keys.

  1. Spend the contrast budget. Cut elements, then put everything saved back into type scale, weight and density. A 23–31px display title over 13px body reads designed; 15px over 14px reads unfinished.
  2. Space groups, unevenly. A uniform mb-6 between every field is a list. Tight inside a cluster, wide between clusters — space replaces the borders.
  3. Tint the neutrals. Chroma-0 grey reads as browser default. A hue held far from the accent (warm ~80 here vs the brand's 245) reads as chosen, and makes the one accent hit harder.
  4. Layer, don't outline. Two or three lightness steps plus one hairline, no shadows. Elevation groups things that a box would have to fence.
  5. One accent, once per view. Colour marks the single most important state on screen and nothing else.
  6. Micro-craft carries it. Tabular numerals, mono for filenames and hotkeys, uppercase micro-labels with real tracking, exact 1px hairlines. This is what "crafted" is made of.
  7. Name the organising idea. Ledger, canvas, statement — below. A screen you cannot name the structure of is stacked fields.
